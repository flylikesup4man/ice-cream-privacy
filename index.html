Privacy Policy

Ice Cream Mobile

Developer: Horus Forge Dev

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Horus Forge Dev operates Ice Cream Mobile. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our mobile game, including information processed through third-party services.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the services and features used, the game may process the following information:

  • Google Play Games player identifiers and authentication information.
  • Gameplay events, level progression, and in-game activity.
  • Device information, app version, and technical diagnostics.
  • Advertising identifiers and ad interaction information.
  • Information you provide when contacting support.

3. Google Play Games

Ice Cream Mobile uses Google Play Games Services for player authentication and supported gaming features. Google processes account and authentication information according to its privacy policy. The game does not require a separate in-game password.

4. Firebase Analytics

The game uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand gameplay activity and improve performance and the user experience. This service may process gameplay events, device identifiers, and app usage information.

5. Advertising

Ice Cream Mobile uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded and interstitial ads.

Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from technical information, and advertising interaction data. Depending on applicable consent and settings, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

6. Game Progress and Local Storage

The game stores gameplay information such as level progression, virtual currency, lives, and booster inventory using device storage and, where enabled, supported game-saving services.

Locally stored information may be removed when app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. Data maintained by external services may have separate retention rules.

7. Third-Party Services

The game integrates third-party services provided by Google. Their privacy practices are described in the following resources:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy Information
  • Google Advertising Technologies

8. Data Sharing

Information may be processed by our service providers, including Google, for authentication, advertising, analytics, and game functionality. We do not sell player information directly to data brokers.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained as needed to provide game functionality, support users, comply with legal obligations, and maintain the security of our services. Third-party providers apply their own retention and deletion practices.

You may contact us to request information about your data or to request deletion of data controlled by Horus Forge Dev. We will review requests and respond in accordance with applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

We aim to comply with applicable privacy requirements concerning children and age-appropriate advertising. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our services. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the game, its services, or legal requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
